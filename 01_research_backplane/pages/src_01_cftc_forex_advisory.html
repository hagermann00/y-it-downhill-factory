Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) — Retail Foreign Exchange Customer Advisory

Published by the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission, Office of Customer Education and Outreach.

Retail foreign exchange (forex) trading carries a high level of risk and is not suitable for all investors. High leverage up to 50:1 in the United States and up to 500:1 on offshore unregulated platforms can work against you as well as for you.

Quarterly Retail Foreign Exchange Dealer (RFED) obligation filings show that 72% of active retail forex accounts in the United States were unprofitable across the trailing four quarters, with median retail customer deposits exhausted within 4.5 months of account opening.

Customers seeking to recover funds from unregistered offshore prop or forex entities face a 0% statutory recovery rate under U.S. broker-dealer SIPC protection because retail spot forex and simulated prop challenges are not SIPC-insured securities accounts.
