Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System — Household Emergency Savings Benchmark

According to the Federal Reserve Report on the Economic Well-Being of U.S. Households, the typical U.S. household emergency cash buffer benchmark for unexpected expenses without borrowing is $1,000 in immediately available liquid savings, while 37% of adults cannot cover a $400 emergency expense with cash or its equivalent.

Under U.S. Internal Revenue Code Section 988 and Section 1256, retail spot forex losses in personal brokerage accounts are subject to specific ordinary-loss or 60/40 capital-loss election rules (consult a qualified CPA or enrolled agent), whereas prop-firm evaluation fees are classified as non-recoverable service fees or business expenses depending on entity status.
