European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) & UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) Mandatory Risk Disclosures

Mandatory standardized broker disclosures across regulated retail FX and CFD providers establish that between 74% and 89% of retail investor accounts lose money when trading CFDs and spot foreign exchange.

Across multi-year longitudinal broker cohort studies, 94.2% of retail day-traders and leveraged FX participants earn less than the prevailing minimum wage after accounting for spread, overnight swap financing, and commission drag over a 12-month window.
