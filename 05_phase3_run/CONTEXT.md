## 1. Role in the Downhill Pipeline
Stage 05 executes an independent phased build of **Phase 3: Solidarity** (**Chapter 7: *The Pivot*** and **Chapter 8: *For the Stubborn Fool Who Must***).

## 2. Canonical Constraints
- **Voice Register:** **Warm Solidarity** — Respectful, pragmatic, constructive. Validates the reader's desire for autonomy while redirecting their capital and analytical energy into high-probability paths.
- **Vocabulary Invariant:** Never use the deprecated word `Sovereign` in Pivot titles. Always use `[PIVOT: THE PRACTICAL PIVOT]`.
- **Chapter 7 (*The Pivot*) Required Elements:**
  - **4 Gate-Scored Practical Pivots** (each evaluated against *the Stake*, *the Wait*, *the Odds*, and *the Payoff*).
  - **The Repurposing Ledger** (salvaging charting literacy, Python/PineScript basics, macroeconomic calendar reading, and risk-sizing discipline).
  - **The Resume Translation Matrix** (converting retail trading experience into hireable B2B/ops competencies).
  - **`[PAGE COMIC — 3D PAPERCRAFT]`:** Full-page 3D papercraft comic strip (`_canon/assets/comics_papercraft/comic_3d_papercraft_strip_ref.jpg` + custom SVG strip) showing the transition from challenge hamster-wheel to real-world cash flow.
- **Chapter 8 (*For the Stubborn Fool Who Must*) Required Elements:**
  - **The Honest Way** & **The Smallest Honest Test** (90-day zero-fee statistical proof protocol).
  - **Hard Stop Rules** (pre-committed capital & time circuit breakers).
  - **6 Hard Do-Nots** & **6 Clean Exit Steps** (subscription purge, chargeback/refund rights, tax-loss harvesting documentation).
  - **Cost/Keep Ledger**, **Watch-For Signals**, **Companion Pamphlet Segue**, and **Callbacks** to Chapters 1–6.
