# Cover spec

The cover's fixed chrome. Generated art is only the hero layer underneath; every word is set by the template.
Source: Claude Design artifact "Y-IT Brand System — canon-accurate v4" (https://claude.ai/artifact/YAo6gpQSySaiQZrA6PLCQi), panels 01, 02, 07, 13. Where this file and that board disagree, the board wins until a ruling changes it.

## Geometry (canon, locked)
- Trim 6 × 9 in; bleed canvas 6.125 × 9.25 in per panel.
- Art may bleed; words never enter the trim buffer ("Ink may bleed. Words may not.").
- Interior is 72–80 pp Standard Colour (canon ruling 2026-08-06), so the spine is about 0.16–0.18 in and carries **no type** (spine text only at 100 pp or more).
- Spine width and full-wrap size come from the designer's final page count (contract `cover-bundle.v1`). Formula to be verified against KDP's current calculator before the first real bundle.

## Front panel
1. **Series banner**, full width at the very top: brick red `#C0392B`, cream uppercase, letter-spaced: `MODERN COMMERCE SERIES • PREMIER CASE STUDY • VOL. NN`.
2. **Wordmark lockup**: `www.` charcoal + `y-it` volt heavy display + `.shop` charcoal, on a cream chip or directly on slate. Cart-and-Y mark is retired.
3. **Hero art** (generated): subject upper-middle; lower 40% kept calm for the title.
4. **Title stack**: `Y-IT: PROBABLY WON'T` large, then `Work` set smaller on the next line ("embarrassed to finish itself"). Metadata string stays `Y-IT: Probably Won't Work`.
5. **Topic line**: the topic name, display face. *(Placement to settle on the first rendered proofs.)*
6. **Subtitle**: cream, centred, the transparent-look hook naming what the first two chapters give away.

## Back panel
- Banner slot reads `THE AUTOPSY`.
- Sales copy (from `copy/`), barcode area bottom right (size to verify against KDP), price.
